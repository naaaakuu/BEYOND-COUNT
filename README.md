# BEYOND COUNT (गणनानन्तरम्)
### An Ancient Indian Science Exhibition Website on Number, Infinity & Deep Time

An interactive web application designed for a Science Exhibition stall. It weaves classical Indian mathematical-philosophical traditions (*Sāṅkhya*, *Asaṅkhyeya*, *Ananta*) with modern particle physics and radiocarbon chronology (*कालगणना*).

---

## 🏛️ Exhibition Features

### 1. The Exhibition Pavilion (Hero Viewport - All On One Screen)
- **Antique Indian Royal Vibe**: Sandstone carved arches, flickering brass *diya* oil lamps, and marigold accents.
- **Three Showcase Vessels**:
  - **Vessel I — COUNTABLE (*साङ्ख्य*)**: Glossy 3D pink spheres with dynamic collision physics, live count display, and interactive shake/add controls.
  - **Vessel II — UNCOUNTABLE (*असंख्येय*)**: Thousands of delicate, gossamer cotton filaments (*resha*) drifting with organic Brownian motion; includes "Puff Air" and "Illuminate" modes.
  - **Vessel III — INFINITE (*अनन्त*)**: An endless pouring cascade of golden sand micro-grains inspired by the ancient metaphor of *Ganga-valuka* (the infinite sands of the holy river Ganga).
- **Ambient Sound Synthesizer**: Fully self-contained meditative Indian Tanpura drone generated via the Web Audio API.

---

### 2. Radiocarbon Chronometry Wing (Below the Stall)
- **Illustrated 4-Stage Cycle**: High-resolution educational diagram explaining how cosmic rays produce Carbon-14, living biomass assimilation, radioactive beta decay upon death, and AMS mass spectrometry.
- **Interactive Atom Decay Chamber**: 180 individual atoms rendered in real-time on HTML5 Canvas, actively transitioning from glowing green $^{14}C$ into sapphire blue $^{14}N$.
- **50,000-Year Timeline Slider**: Real-time evaluation of half-lives ($t_{1/2} = 5,730\text{ years}$), remaining percentage, and archaeological horizons.
- **Historic Indian Artifact Presets**:
  - *Bakhshali Manuscript* (~1,720 yr — earliest recorded symbol of zero, Oxford radiocarbon dated)
  - *Ashokan Palisade* (~2,320 yr — Maurya capital wooden architecture)
  - *Rakhigarhi Harappan Relic* (~4,500 yr — Mature Indus Valley timber)
  - *Bhimbetka Caves* (~10,000 yr — Prehistoric rock shelter charcoal pigments)
